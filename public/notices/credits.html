Credits and limitations

Inspired by Light Teachings by Kia Preston (pkncoder), reference snapshot.

The integration code and original scene were AI-assisted implementations of the team's supplied mathematical specification. No upstream source was copied or adapted by AI 3. AI 1's integrated WebGL renderer reports independent implementation with no copied upstream code; its detailed provenance is retained in the source handoff. Future UI/source reuse must be reviewed and credited before release.

This is not a pixel-for-pixel reproduction. Ray tracing is an established technique, not our invention. Version 1 supports analytic spheres/planes, direct illumination, hard shadows and perfect mirrors, with at most four redirects. No glass, global illumination, soft shadows or progressive sampling.

This checkpoint displays an actual WebGL2 image with CPU-computed inspection events and a low-resolution CPU fallback. The renderer/core combination has passed local browser and sampled pixel-parity checks. The development harness is not the finished educational interface: the complete controls, keyboard pixel selection and explanatory diagram still await AI 2. Other browsers/devices and public hosting remain unverified.
