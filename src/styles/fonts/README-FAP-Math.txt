FAP Math (fap-math.woff2)
=========================

A modified version of Latin Modern Math 1.959 (GUST e-foundry), as distributed on
CTAN: https://ctan.org/pkg/lm-math  (unmodified original: fonts/lm-math/opentype/latinmodern-math.otf)

Modifications, made for Follow a Pixel's "mathematics" chapter:
  - Subset to the characters the app typesets (Basic Latin, Latin-1, Greek,
    general punctuation including primes and invisible operators, letterlike
    symbols, arrows, mathematical operators, miscellaneous technical,
    supplemental operators, mathematical bold/italic Latin and Greek).
  - The OpenType MATH table (stretchy constructions, size variants and math
    constants) is kept, so native MathML renders with Computer Modern metrics.
  - Converted to WOFF2 and renamed to "FAP Math", following clause 1 of the
    GUST Font License, so it cannot be mistaken for the original.
